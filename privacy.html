The legal basis for our use of GitHub Pages, where personal data is involved, is our legitimate interest in providing a secure and reliable website under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Further information is available in GitHub’s information about data collection for GitHub Pages and the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand aggregate website usage and improve performance and content. According to Cloudflare, this privacy-focused service does not use cookies and does not collect or use personal data for analytics. It provides aggregated metrics such as page views and referring websites.

More information is available in Cloudflare’s Web Analytics documentation.

5. Contact by email

If you contact us by email, we process the information you provide, including your email address and message, to answer your request. Depending on the subject, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for pre-contractual or service-related communication, or Article 6(1)(f) GDPR for our legitimate interest in providing support.

Messages are retained only for as long as necessary to resolve the request and comply with legal record-keeping duties.

7. Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising cookies, profiling cookies, or a consent-based tracking system. Basic browser functionality such as copying the add-on code does not require persistent storage.

To exercise your rights, contact help.synapse.pro@gmail.com.

We may update this privacy policy when the website, service providers, or legal requirements change. The current version is always published on this page.
